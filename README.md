# 💖 "Create Your Birthday Surprise" Platform

A fully customizable, romantic digital platform that allows **ANY user** to create, customize, preview, save, and share an interactive birthday surprise website for their girlfriend—without editing any code!

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Romantic Setup Wizard ("Create Your Birthday Surprise ❤️")**:
   - **Step 1 — Her Details 💕**: Name, nickname, birthday date & time, profile picture upload, favorite color picker, short bio.
   - **Step 2 — Your Relationship ❤️**: Your name, date met, place met, how met, first date, anniversary, favorite memory (with **automatic auto-hide** for empty optional fields!).
   - **Step 3 — Memories & Timeline 📸⏳**: Upload multiple Polaroid photos with captions & dates, plus customizable relationship timeline events.
   - **Step 4 — Messages & Letters 💌**: Love letter editor with real-time live preview, "Open When..." envelopes manager (add/edit/delete categories), "Reasons I Love You" manager.
   - **Step 5 — Quiz, Final Gift & Security 🎁🎵**: Custom 4-option quiz builder, 3D gift box text, secret easter egg message, password protection toggle, and custom MP3 song upload.
   - **Step 6 — Theme Customization & Live Preview 🎨👀**: 5 predefined themes (🌹 Romantic, 🌌 Midnight, 🌸 Soft Pink, ✨ Elegant, 💜 Dreamy) with live preview, auto-save, and shareable link generation.

2. **Returning User Dashboard ("Welcome Back ❤️")**:
   - Save multiple surprise profiles in browser `localStorage`.
   - Create new surprises, switch active surprises, edit, or delete surprises.

3. **Shareable Link Generator 🔗**:
   - Encodes your customization into a shareable URL hash payload (`#data=...`) so anyone opening your link sees your customized romantic surprise instantly!

4. **All Interactive Surprise Features Maintained**:
   - Password Gate 🔐
   - Birthday Countdown ⏳
   - Open When Envelopes 💌
   - Polaroid Memories Gallery 📸
   - Love Meter ❤️ (overflows past 100%)
   - Reasons I Love You Card Flipper 💕
   - Mini Relationship Quiz 👀
   - 3D Glowing Gift Box 🎁
   - Canvas Fireworks 🎆 & Particle Confetti Streams
   - Floating Background Music 🎵

## 🚀 How to Use & Deploy

1. Open [index.html](file:///c:/Users/kumar/OneDrive/Desktop/birthdaysurprise/index.html) in your browser.
2. Click **Customize Surprise ✏️** in the top right floating menu to launch the setup wizard.
3. Fill in her details, upload photos, write your letter, customize the quiz & theme.
4. Click **Save & Open My Surprise ❤️** or **Share Link 🔗**!
5. Deploy to [Vercel](https://vercel.com) by dragging and dropping this folder!
